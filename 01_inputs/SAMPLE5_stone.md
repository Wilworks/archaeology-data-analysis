# Sample 5 (scan label 491)

Identified by the owner of the specimen as a **stone**; it is not reconstructed.

* Screenshot (3D Slicer volume rendering): `04_figures/00_data_views/sample5_stone_screenshot.png` - a smooth, pale, egg-shaped object with a very even surface and one small pit,
  surrounded by loose brown fragments; a flat striated plate touches it on the right.
* Scan data known from the first draft (Table 2): 67,438 voxels at 500 HU in the present extraction (75,587 in Table 2), mean 1505 HU (higher than every ceramic piece).
  No point file was extracted for it.
* INAA (first draft, Figures 2 and 3): Al 9.48 wt %, correlations 0.971-0.985 with Samples 1, 3 and 4 - i.e. it groups with the ceramics. Either the stone is clay-rich (laterite-like)
  or the grouping and the wording "five ceramic artifacts" need rethinking. Left for the authors (see 07_manuscript/TO_VERIFY.md).
* A smooth water-worn pebble may have been used for burnishing pottery; that is a possibility to check against the archaeological record, not a finding.
